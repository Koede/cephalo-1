What is in Cephalo-2
  • Arms: rev 4 tentacles, 8 servo joints each, clutches, winch line, a camera in every tip bay.
  • Hands: hand3 on all eight arms (four fingers that are also a 9 in rotor), with the current hub: hub v3, a self-locking winch per finger (each finger curls on its own, wound by static drivers under the hub) and the S3 cam sleeve that turns all four fingers between four grip layouts. Shown in the flight cross. The "older hand3" button shows the Nov 15 hub.
  • Bayonet connector on every tip (orange: arm socket, dark: the shared hand base). Quarter-turn, 3 lugs, 5 spring pins. Drawn on the tip axis; every hand sits 9.9 mm further out than before.
  • Belly rotor: 4-blade, 22 in Scabbard rotor in place of the EDF. Standing, the blades are stowed upright in the turret; flying, they are out.
  • Turret ("bucket"): scabbard cage, elevator, fold rods, dock latch pins, and 5 cameras (4 side, 1 up) behind clear windows. No eyestalks.
