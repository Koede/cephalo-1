## Control: one body (body4, adopted 2026-11-11; details in `claude/body-controller.md`)
One continuous, event-driven controller replaces the per-behaviour timelines. It is driven by intents (get up, idle, walk any direction + turn, wave, take off, fly to x/y/z/heading, land, lie down). Every skill starts from the measured state and ends on a sensed condition.
- **Reflexes:** sensed lift-off; ToF final approach + felt touchdown; terrain absorption (touching feet become stance feet where they landed); a load governor (skills slow to 0.45 × above 0.80 × stall or on claw-prop saturation); weight shift before lifting a leg.
- **Spinal layer:** a 2nd-order tracker on every joint (120°/s, 800°/s² loaded; 180, 1200 airborne) makes every transition continuous.
- **One thrust law:** ground collective → altitude loop with feed-forward; gains blend. Thrust ceilings follow the pack voltage (`CEPH_POWER=1`, default); TakeOff refuses when the sagged ceilings cannot carry it (> 92 % on 8 / > 100 % on 6 claw-props). Gains are a trainable dict (`Body.g`).
- **Full session** (lying → get up → walk 18 cm + 30° → wave → take off → fly north/climb → fly east/descend/turn 90° → land → lie down) completes at mid **and pessimistic** thrust **with an ideal supply (`CEPH_POWER=0`)**. Worst servo 0.86 × stall, slip ≤ 0.4°, tilt ≤ 1.0°, flight path error ≤ 7 cm, up to 37 joints moving at once. With battery sag and 6 in claw-props, take-off is refused. (These results are from before the senses; see the section above.)
- **Unexpected 12 cm step:** the robot lands on it gently (0.82 × stall).
- **Legs 0 + 4 always move together** (their mass shifts cancel). This is what makes the pessimistic thrust flyable.
- **Eyes are not in body4 yet:** no gaze skill, no tuck reflex (open issue below).

## Poses and modes (degrees, j0..j7)
- **Stand:** [−11.7, 0, 39.2, 0, 64.0, 0, −1.5, 0], plate 300 mm above the floor, feet at r 360 mm, claw fold 60° (tripod fingertips). Winch tight. In body4 the body breathes (±4 mm height, 5 mm sway) on fixed feet. **Upside down, the same pose is the inverted flight pose.**
- **Guard (proposed 2026-11-12, posed only; `tipcams4.py`, `tipcams.md`):** lying on the EDF bumper, the arms close over the dome in two tiers like a bud and the eyestalks are tucked inside; the 8 tip cameras keep watch (100 % of the horizon band). Even arms [−17, 0, −100, 0, −10, 0, 10, 0], odd arms [−11, 0, −50, 0, −90, 0, −10, 0]; ≥ 12 mm to the dome and the tucked stalks, ≥ 10 mm arm to arm, 0.21 × stall static. The path into and out of it is not planned.
- **Tripod stand on the Trivet rotor (proposed 2026-11-11; `trivet.py`, details in `claude/trivet-rotor.md`, video `cephalo1_trivet_rotor.mp4`, kinematic):**
  - The robot stands on its folded belly rotor (plate 295 mm, feet r 230 mm) with **all 8 arms free**, no thrust and no holding power. Tips at 15° with all arms raised (12 N·m overturning moment).
  - **Hand-over:** after a normal landing the arms hold the plate at 320 mm ([−6.0, 0, 38.9, 0, 59.6, 0, −4.1, 0], 0.28 × stall static), the rotor screws its legs down (5.6 backward turns), the body lowers 25 mm onto them, the arms lift off.
  - **Parked:** the arm toes rest on the floor (1 mm pre-press) so the floor carries the arms. Holding XL pods up in the tulip pose without thrust costs 0.40 × stall at j0 (0.88-0.92 × rated rms).
  - **Clearances:** arms to the blades' swept surface 37 mm during the hand-over, 44 mm when walking with the blades parked at 6° droop, 136 mm in the tulip flight pose, 133 mm in the candle pose.
  - **Leg loads (statics):** link 249 N static / 747 N with all weight on one leg; blade airfoil root 20 / 59 MPa (PA-CF 80 MPa est.).
- **Take-off from the tripod on the claw-props (2026-11-12; `tripod4.py`, MuJoCo full robot, details in `claude/trivet-rotor.md`, video `cephalo1_tripod_takeoff.mp4`):**
  - **Needs XL claw-props.** With 6 in claw-props the robot (10.61 kg, 104 N) needs 137 % of their sagged ceiling (101 N even with an ideal supply), so the self-check refuses.
  - **Sequence:** rest → arms unfurl (toes lift 70 mm, root-to-tip unfold, pairs 1+5 → 2+6 → 3+7 → 0+4, 0.3 s apart, 2.0 s swing, no sideways curl) → claw-props unload the legs → sensed lift-off at 5.6 s → 40 cm climb → the rotor unfolds in the air (5.6 forward turns in 2.4 s, toes 384 mm up) → spin-up to 4144 rpm in 2.6 s → hover on rotor 45.7 N + 8 × 8.2 N by 12.8 s → 50 cm climb.
  - **Yaw:** the rotor motor's reaction torque (≤ 2.4 N·m in spin-up, ~2.0 in hover) is held by leaning every claw axis tangentially through j7 (4.5° in hover); heading error ≤ 3.7°.
  - **Results** (base / pessimistic 80 % thrust / 60 % charge): worst servo 0.51 × stall (arm unfurl), clutch slip ≤ 0.2°, tilt ≤ 0.1°, altitude error ≤ 2.5 cm, no claw-prop saturated (lift-off at 52 / 65 / 56 % of ceiling), min pack 22.8 / 22.7 / 20.8 V. Clearances: blades to arms ≥ 97 mm, arms to floor ≥ 144 mm during the unfurl, arms to body ≥ 19 mm.
  - Not yet a body4 skill; landing onto the tripod not yet simulated.
- **Walk:** ripple gait, one foot at a time (7 down), 90 mm stride, 45 mm lift, 0.30 s swing in 0.36 s slots; fans and EDF off.
  - Mission sim: order 0, 4, 2, 6, 1, 5, 3, 7, worst servo 1.0 × stall momentarily (j6 in swings), rms 0.82 × rated (0.78 before the senses).
  - body4: any direction with a turn, each foot stepping to where it should be half a cycle later, worst servo 0.82 × stall. The asked distance and turn are overshot by about 10 % (open-loop between skills).
- **Mid-air flip + walking upside down (drafted 2026-11-11; `flip4.py`, details in `claude/flip-and-inverted-walk.md`):**
  - **Gimballed roll:** 180° in 4 s about the axis through arms 0 + 4. The arms keep their claw-prop axes vertical while the body turns over: 0 + 4 twist, 1-3 go over the top, 5-7 swing under with props stopped.
  - **Roll path:** solved offline for vertical axes, joint load, thrust centroid over the CoM, and clearances.
  - **Then:** inverted hover, landing leg pair by pair, ripple walk in a "spider" stance ([43, 0, −71, 0, −80, 0, 18, 0], knees up, plate 0.32 m, feet r 0.30 m, dome 16 cm clear).
  - **Needs:** XL claw-props, 1 pack, STS3250 at j1.
  - **Results:**
    - roll: 0.94 × stall, attitude error ≤ 7.7°, 7 cm altitude dip, claw-props at their ceiling for 1.1 s;
    - landing: 0.77 × stall;
    - walk: 18.2 cm + 20.3° asked 18 cm + 20°, 0.99 × stall for 2 frames (95th percentile 0.68);
    - clutch slip ≤ 0.2° throughout.
  - **Walk fixes:** a touchdown latch (feet stay where they actually landed) and the load governor off for this gait.
  - **Eyestalks:** tuck them (or keep |pan| ≤ 25°) for the roll and the spider stance: in that stance an eye swung to pan −35°, tilt +35° is 9.6 mm from an arm. The roll path (arms 1-3 over the top) has not been checked against the stalks.
- **Heavy falls and recovery (drafted and reinforced 2026-11-11; corrected and re-run 2026-11-12; pivot take-off 2026-11-13; `fall4.py`, details in `claude/falls-and-recovery.md`, videos `cephalo1_pivot_takeoff.mp4`, `cephalo1_falls_reinforced.mp4`, `cephalo1_kickstand_takeoff.mp4`):**
  - **Correction (2026-11-12):** the first reinforced video drew the arms through the floor and unrecovered. The renderer read the joint angles with the rev 4 layout (no j1 clutch); the sim also used 6 in claw geometry and link contact capsules slimmer than the drawn parts. All three are fixed (`fall_video.py`/`fall_checks.py` assert the layout; `fall4.py` simulates the XL claw size and a 31.5 mm link contact radius; `pen_chk.py` checks the drawn geometry against the floor: ≤ 7 mm). The numbers below are from the re-run.
  - **Reflexes:**
    - On a power loss or tumble the arms go to a stand-like brace (feet 0.30 m below the plate, 0.36 m out), with emergency joint limits of 230°/s and 2500°/s². The winch is soft for the fall.
    - After it settles, any clutch that skipped a notch is **re-indexed**: the servo drives toward the link's own stop until the Hall switch reports home. Then the winch ramps soft → firm over 1.2 s.
    - Recovery by how it came to rest: **GetUp** (belly-down, tilt < 50°), **pivot take-off** (dome-down, tilt ≥ 140°) or thrust righting (on its side, 50-140°: reverse flip path; not seen in any run so far). A **notch watchdog** restarts the recovery if a roller skips again; the repeated get-up holds the winch tight.
  - **Pivot take-off (2026-11-13, `FALL_STYLE=pivot`, default):**
    - The arms rise root-first with props off. All 8 props then run at 45 → 60 % of the weight and the attitude reference leans 10° and sweeps round at 0.5 turns/s, so the body rolls round in a circle on its dome (about 1.3 turns). The circle stops with the low side at the middle "under" arm of the roll path.
    - That arm stops its prop and **pops out as one strut** in 0.55 s (j0 −48°, the rest out to the side, standing on its j2 housing 0.23 m from the body axis). Thrust rises to 85 %; the dome leaves the floor and **the body leans on the leg** (27-30 N).
    - **It pivots off the leg:** the roll upright starts (4.0 s, about the axis through the two arms 90° from the leg), the leg keeps its direction on the floor (j0 follows the roll), the far side swings up and over; about 32° of roll with the leg loaded. The leg then lets go and folds onto the roll path, and the roll and the climb to 0.9 m finish together.
    - **With the 190 g budget pods, 11 dome-down runs: 11 first-try take-offs, recovery gear 0.67-0.81, 15.2-18.5 s** (old undirected lift-off: 8 of 11 first try, up to 1.42, 13-44 s). With the new 156 g pods: see "New pods" below. Mid-roll five props carry the robot: at their 28 N ceiling for 0.6 s, attitude error up to 19°. Arm-body clearance ≥ 4 mm; arm tips brush the floor mid-roll (drawn −5 / −8 mm).
    - **Wobble style (`FALL_STYLE=wobble`, 2026-11-12):** same circle, but the leg props the body level (11.5° → 1.5°), lift-off straight up on 7 props (23-25 N), 0.14 m hop, leg folds, climb, then the roll: 11 of 11, 0.60-0.81, 18.5-22.4 s.

    - The rev 4 clutches overload the gears in tumbles because the links hit the cam stops. Ride-over cams remove the stop, so the load moves into the link stops (structure). Those stops and the pivot bearing pairs must be designed for it (above).
    - The 45° tilt failsafe caused the hit crash while 24 N·m of correction was still available → **no tilt failsafe; cut the motors only on a confirmed crash.**
  - **Eyestalks in falls (not simulated):** the eye back-drives its tilt servo above about 22 g and the body sees 24-59 g, so the stalks are TPU and should tuck as a fall reflex; tucked eyes still stand 45 mm above the crown, so dome-down landings and the pivot take-off (which circles on the dome) hit them first.
- **Wave (body4):** shift the body 2.5 cm away, lift the foot 6 cm straight up, raise the arm and run a travelling yaw/pitch wave root → tip at 0.8 Hz (slowed to stay inside the STS3215 torque-speed line); 0.76 × stall.
- **Take-off (v7, organic + fast, adopted 2026-11-10; `sim4.py` default, `CEPH_TAKEOFF=v7`):**
  - EDF spools up (min-jerk, 0.6 s), then the leg pairs unfurl in a wave rotating around the body, 1+5 → 2+6 → 3+7, 0.4 s apart.
  - Each arm unfurls root to tip (each pitch joint starts 6 % of the swing after the one before). The yaw joints j3/j5 curl it sideways by up to 9°/6° (same rotational sense on every arm, back to 0 at the flight pose), so the legs spiral open.
  - Swing 1.55 s. A leg in the air may run at 180°/s and 1200°/s² (2/3 of the STS3215's 270°/s no-load speed at 12 V). The foot lift and unfold overlap, so the leg never pauses.
  - Claw-props fade in over 0.4 s; the collective unloads the last feet in 0.6 s; lift-off blends gains over 0.4 s. Winch firm. 4.4 s from EDF start to the last leg up at mid thrust.
  - **Legs 0 + 4 unfurl together after lift-off** (body4: up to a 97 % six-claw-prop load; with the senses that is 81 % at mid and 101 % at pessimistic thrust, so the pessimistic take-off is now refused). `sim4.py` still uses the old rule: together only up to 85 %, otherwise one after the other; with the senses its pessimistic take-off tips over (it had only completed because the body sank until the EDF can touched the floor).
  - With XL pods (+75 g per tip) the landing reaches 1.00 × stall and 17° clutch slip: open.
  - v6 (`CEPH_TAKEOFF=v6`): same robot, symmetric pairs 0.9 s apart, 1.9 s swings at 120°/s, sequential legs 4 then 0: 7.8 s.
  - v5 (`CEPH_TAKEOFF=v5`): pairs one after another, each leg stopping twice at keyframes, j4 commanded at 334°/s (beyond the servo), 25° on/off fans, hop + sequential legs 4 then 0: 10.7 s.
- **Flight ("tulip"):** [−6.9, 0, −25.8, 0, −57.0, 0, −0.3, 0], claw axes vertical, hubs at r 360 mm, 353 mm above the plate; arm-body clearance 106 mm; prop-disc gap 123 mm. Hover: EDF 80 % + 8.7 N per claw-prop (8.3 N before the senses; body4 keeps the EDF at 100 %: 7.2 N per claw-prop before the senses). Winch firm. - **Quad mode (drafted 2026-11-11; `quad4.py`, details in `claude/quad-mode-and-power.md`):** arms 0/2/4/6 fly in a "candle" pose ([5.6, 0, −63.4, 0, −45.1, 0, 12.9, 0], hubs r 0.30 m, 0.36 m up; 0.41 × stall at hover thrust) while 1 + 3 dig with a shovel and 5 + 7 scrub a pan, at the same time. - **Landing (v7, organic, adopted 2026-11-11; `claude/organic-landing.md`; body4 adds sensing):**
  - One continuous movement. The body sinks on one minimum-jerk curve while legs 0 + 4 reach down together (take-off unfurl reversed). From touch-down the pairs follow in a rotating wave (3+7 → 2+6 → 1+5, 0.4 s apart), claw-props fade out as their arms curl down, and gains blend flight → ground. 5.1 s from hover to the last foot down (v6: 13.1 s).
  - **body4:** the feet's ToF sees the ground and re-times legs + body for a gentle arrival. Felt contact starts the wave; the pairs are guided by IK onto the real ground; the body then stands up to full height.

- **Organic mid-air correction (drafted 2026-11-10; `flight4.py`, details in `claude/organic-midair-correction.md`):**
  - "Tentacle vectoring": the body stays level, and position and yaw are corrected by tilting each claw-prop axis up to 18°.

## Conventions and environment
