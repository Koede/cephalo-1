# hand3 flight check (2026-11-14)

**Superseded in part the same day: the hand was shrunk to rev D (9 in rotor, 10 servos, 495 g) and the grasp is now simulated with finger contact. Current numbers: `claude/hand3-revD.md`. Everything below is rev C (10 in, 570 g) and, for hand work mode, the tie model.**

## Flight check with hand3 on arms 2 and 6 (2026-11-14, MuJoCo, `hand3_fly.py` + `body4h.py`)
Full robot, current build (XL claw-props on the other six arms, 2 packs, STS3250 at j1, senses on): **12.66 kg, W 124 N** (11.84 kg with XL pods on all eight). Hand model: 570 g per hand (COM 45 mm), 10 in disc, blade plane 92 mm out, ceilings 21.3 / 23.35 / 25.4 N at 22.2 V scaled (V/22.2)^1.5, 10.1 W/N^1.5, ESC 60 A. Programs: liftoff, short, day (get up, walk, wave, take-off, north, east + turn, land, lie down). Estimates throughout: thrust is BEMT, no spin test.
- **Thrust holds at all three levels (low / mid / high).** Take-off passes the self-check (hover load 0.41 on eight props, 0.55 on six); hover is 11.0-11.3 N per hand, i.e. 52-55 % of the low ceiling (47-50 % at mid), EDF 37.2 N.
- Peak hand thrust in the day mission (flight leg, east + turn): 82-87 % of the low ceiling, 75-79 % mid, 69-73 % high. Landing 57-65 % (low). The hand arms never saturate.
- Saturation seen: XL claw-props on arms 1 and 5 (the neighbours of the hands) reach their ceiling in the take-off wave and the landing, as in the all-XL baseline; servo j0 of arm 0 and the loaded legs reach 1.0 × stall there (governor slows to 0.45), also as in the baseline. Hover itself has margin everywhere.
- Electrical: pack peak 235 A (baseline 202 A), min bus 22.5 V, EDF 1.02 × its ceiling at the loaded voltage (baseline too), ESC peaks 33 A (XL arms) and 24 A (hands, rated 60 A). SOC 100 → 92 % over the liftoff program.
- Tilt: 2.6° take-off, 0.7° cruise, 13.7° for 0.5 s in the landing (baseline 6.4°); it settles and the lie-down is clean.
- **Finding (needs a design rule): with the standard tentacle steering, leaning the heavy hand arms for the east + turn leg overshoots their claw-axis cone; both hand props cut out and the robot fell, at every hand thrust level (not a thrust shortfall).** Fix used: the hand arms stay upright in flight (no lean, thrust only) and the other six steer. All three levels then fly the full day mission.
- **Spin handedness (new rule):** the old SPIN put hands 2 and 6 the same way, so their torque adds and the robot flips once real rotor q/T is used. Use SPIN `[1,-1,-1,1,-1,1,1,-1]` with **mirror-image (opposite-handed) hand3 rotors**; 8-prop, 6-prop and 4-prop sums are all zero.
- **Rotor q/T measured:** 0.0297 / 0.0274 / 0.0250 m (low/mid/high, from hand3_thrust_cad.json) vs 0.0173 for the XL pod. All three levels still fly liftoff, step and day with it.
- **Heel stance: closed.** XL foot_drop 0.1103 m vs hand heel 0.110 m (0.3 mm), stance IK unchanged.
- **monitor4 restored** with per-arm hand ratings (`monitor4.py`). Hand-induced flags: slip 1.08 on landing (arm 2 j2); tip_Nm 0.97-1.18 of target on the hand arms; link_MPa fine (0.16-0.19). Touchdown pushes the hands to 0.97-1.0 of their ceiling for ~0.1 s at every level. Pre-existing in the baseline: thermal 1.4-1.5, pigtail, EDF 1.02, XL props on arms 1 and 5 saturating. Joint-force 1.45 at t=0.04 s on arm 2 j0 is a start-up artifact.
- **Quad mode (four props fly, four arms work shovel + pan + brush at once): not feasible with hand3.** Hands as flyers (arms 0,2,4,6) need ~24 N per prop vs ceilings 21.3 / 23.35 / 25.4 N. Role swap (XL claw-props 1,3,5,7 fly, hands work): 12.66 kg + tools needs 23-26 N per flyer with the EDF at 37 N, i.e. 83-91 % of the 28 N cap; no room left for attitude, the robot sinks 45 cm, flyer clutches slip (sat 0.86, J 53, same at all hand levels). Moving the work set to recentre the COM does not fix it (J 46-48). (Correction: an earlier note here said "112 N vs 106 N"; that left out the EDF. The limit is control margin, not total thrust.)
- **Replacement: hand work mode (`quad4r.py`, `Q_TASK=shovel|dishes`). The two hand3 hands (arms 2 and 6) do one chore at a time and the six XL claw-props + EDF fly.** Opposite workers keep the flyer set symmetric. Flyers use the spread flight pose (52 mm rim-to-rim between neighbours; the candle pose leaves 17 mm). Thrust allocation evens out thrust/ceiling over the six flyers (reweighted least squares; the plain pseudo-inverse saturated the flyer next to the workers).
  - Shovel (hand 2 upper grip, hand 6 lower grip, shaft passes 80 mm beside the EDF axis): J 5.2 (old quad baseline 5.5), position error mean 1.5 / max 2.9 cm, height error 1.3 cm, tilt 2.5 deg, no saturation, flyers ~15 N mean, peak 0.89 of ceiling, servo max 0.82, tool tie 4 mm. Monitor: thermal 1.18 (arm 2 j4), EDF at its ceiling; rest ok, tip coupler 0.64 N m.
  - Dishes (hand 2 holds the pan 0.20 m off axis, clear of the EDF jet; hand 6 scrubs): J 7.3, error mean 1.3 / max 2.9 cm, height 1.9 cm, tilt 1.9 deg, no saturation, peak flyer 0.92 of ceiling. Flags: servo 1.00 at the start (arm 2 j1) and thermal 1.58 on the brush arm (arm 6 j2; the old quad baseline has 1.41), tie 17 mm, tip coupler 2.9 N m. Lowering the pan to -0.42 m fails; -0.27 m is no better.
  - Hand thrust level does not matter here (the hands do not spin). Gains are the old quad-trained set, not retrained. Grasp is the tie model at the heel point; no finger contact model.
- **Folded fingers (estimate from the CAD part masses):** the four fingers are ~26 g of moving mass (phalanges + pads 6.4 g each) of the 570 g hand. Fully folded they move the hand COM <= 2.5 mm along the axis, <= 0.015 N m at the tip coupler. Parked rotor has no centrifugal load. Closed as negligible.
